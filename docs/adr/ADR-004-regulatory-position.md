# ADR-004 Regulatory position and process rigour

## Context

The products are intended for worldwide use beyond the United States. The owner states that they are not medical devices or regulated maintenance record systems, and that standards such as IEC 62304 and DO-178C do not apply; the hackathon build is a prototype for proof of utility with no real-world use (owner, 2026-11-10).

## Options

1. Follow a light process because no regulation applies.
2. Follow a controlled, traceable process voluntarily because the activities are high-risk, while recording the regulatory position as the owner's.

## Decision

Option 2 (owner, 2026-11-10). The regulatory position is recorded as the owner's (DOC-010 §4). These documents make no other classification or standards claim. Before real-world use, the position is confirmed for each country of use (OD-029).

## Consequences

- DOC-000 to DOC-100 apply in full to both products.
- Real-world use is blocked until OD-029 and the other `real-world use` decisions in DOC-003 are closed.
